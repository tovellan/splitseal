# Governance

SplitSeal starts with a maintainer-led governance model.

Maintainers review changes, manage releases, respond to security reports, and preserve
the project's privacy and compatibility contracts. Decisions should be recorded in pull
requests or issues unless they contain security-sensitive information.

Changes require review from at least one maintainer other than the author when the
maintainer group has more than one active member. Cryptographic format, redaction, and
canonicalization changes require two maintainer approvals when two are available.

Regular contributors may be invited as maintainers after a sustained record of sound
technical work, careful review, and responsible handling of security concerns. A
maintainer may step down at any time. Inactive maintainers may be moved to emeritus
status after six months without project activity.
