# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Participants must communicate professionally, discuss technical work in good faith, and
avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, or disclosure of another person's
private information. Critique decisions and code, not people.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository security contact when public
discussion would expose a reporter or affected person. Maintainers may edit or remove
content, restrict participation, or ban participants when necessary to protect the
project community. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately,
and avoid unnecessary disclosure.
